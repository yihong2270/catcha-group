About us

Our history

Since 1999, Catcha Group has been building and backing category-defining businesses across Southeast Asia. We have a proven history of seeing opportunities early, moving fast, and executing with discipline — from launching market-leading platforms to taking companies public. Over two decades, we've operated through every market cycle, scaling ideas into industry leaders and building a reputation for turning bold visions into real outcomes.

Our approach today

Today, we're a family office that is operated by its principals. We are focused on finding extraordinary businesses where we can drive real, hands-on impact.

We don't just invest — we get deeply involved, working side-by-side with founders to unlock growth, sharpen strategy, and build long-term value. We invest our own capital in every deal, standing shoulder-to-shoulder with the teams we back. Our approach is simple: we only bet where we are willing to put our own money, energy, and expertise on the line.
